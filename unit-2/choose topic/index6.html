Editorial Geometry

Bending around spheres. Modern web typography is no longer locked into rigid grids. By applying geometric curves to floated objects, paragraph layout streams bend inward natively. The text dynamically recalculates its character break patterns along the computed perimeter, allowing copy to wrap fluidly around round frames without requiring arbitrary spacer blocks or absolute positioning tricks.

Sliding down diagonals. Polygon point coordinates let you build irregular geometric angles, like diagonals and multi-point star patterns. As the content encounters the sharp slope of the polygon vector boundary, individual character clusters stack cleanly along the hypotenuse. This mirrors high-end editorial layouts found in print print advertisements and physical fashion magazines.

Flowing past silhouettes. Beyond predictable circles and straight vectors, browsers can parse alpha transparency channels directly out of an image file. When a PNG with a clear alpha background is evaluated, the text layout tracking maps straight onto the edge of the opaque pixels, letting copy wrap tightly around organic silhouettes like stars, physical products, or detailed portraits.
