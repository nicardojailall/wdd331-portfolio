Scroll-Driven Animations

Scroll down to watch the top progress bar scrub smoothly and cards reveal as they enter the viewport—powered by pure CSS.

1. Scroll Timeline

animation-timeline: scroll() ties keyframe progress directly to the scroll container instead of a fixed clock duration.

2. View Timeline

animation-timeline: view() tracks an element as it moves through the scrollport so reveals sync naturally with scrolling.

3. Animation Range

animation-range: entry 0% cover 35% controls exactly when the reveal starts and finishes relative to the viewport.

4. Reduced Motion

Wrapping motion in @media (prefers-reduced-motion: no-preference) keeps the page accessible for users who prefer static layouts.

5. Capstone Ready

Use subtle view timelines for section headers or feature cards in your unit-7/reimagined-home/ build without writing scroll JS.
